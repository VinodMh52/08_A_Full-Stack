About KBT College of Engineering

Empowering Students Through Quality Education & Innovation

Who We Are

KBT College of Engineering, Nashik, is one of the leading engineering institutions committed to providing quality technical education. The college offers undergraduate engineering programs with modern laboratories, experienced faculty members, and excellent placement support.

Our mission is to develop skilled engineers with strong technical knowledge, leadership qualities, innovation, and ethical values to contribute to society.

🎯 Vision

To become a centre of excellence in engineering education, research and innovation by producing globally competent engineers.

🚀 Mission

Provide quality education, promote research, encourage innovation, and prepare students for successful professional careers.
